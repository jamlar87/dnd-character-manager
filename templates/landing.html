What is on the table

Everything below is part of the app today — no roadmap promises.

📜

Character sheets that keep up

All six ability scores, saves, skills, spell slots, conditions, hit dice and rests — updated live during play, with a level-up wizard that knows the rules.

🎲

DM tools behind the screen

Campaigns, encounters, an initiative tracker and NPCs. Build a fight, place it on the map and run it without a second notebook.

🗺️

A map the table can see

Upload a battle map, align a 5-ft grid to it, keep fog over what the party has not reached, and cast it to a second screen — the player view only ever shows what they have seen.

📚

Searchable manuals

Races, classes, subclasses, spells, monsters and magic items carry their book and page, and the manual search is one box in the navigation bar.

📄

Import and export

Bring in a character you already have — a PDF sheet or JSON — and export your own as JSON or PDF whenever you want it outside the app.

👥

Bring the whole party

Multi-user accounts with a shared campaign: the party's characters, NPCs, maps and encounters travel together as one file.

How it goes

1

Make an account

An email and a password. Your characters are yours — nothing is shared until you share it.

2

Roll it up

Six steps: race, class, abilities, skills, details, review. Randomize or take the ideal default at every one, or import a sheet you already have.

3

Take it to the table

The sheet stays live while you play, and the DM's encounter tracker runs the fight from the other side of the screen.
