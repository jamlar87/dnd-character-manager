Passive Checks
A passive check is a special ability check without a roll — the average result for a task done repeatedly (like searching for secret doors), or when the DM wants to secretly determine success without dice (noticing a hidden monster).

Darkvision
See in darkness within radius as if dim light, and dim light as if bright light. Cannot discern color in darkness — only shades of gray.

Blindsight
Perceive surroundings without sight, within a specific radius. Creatures without eyes (oozes, grimlocks) or with echolocation (bats, dragons) typically have this sense.

Tremorsense
Detect and pinpoint vibrations within a radius, so long as both creature and source touch the same ground or substance. Cannot detect flying or incorporeal creatures.

Truesight
See in normal and magical darkness, see invisible creatures and objects, auto-detect visual illusions (and succeed on saves against them), perceive shapechangers' original forms, and see into the Ethereal Plane — all within a specific range.
