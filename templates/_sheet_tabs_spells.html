▸ How Pact Magic works (PHB p.107)

Pact Magic works differently from other spellcasting. You have a small number of spell slots that you regain on a short or long rest, not just a long rest.

Always upcast. All your Pact Magic slots are the same level. Any spell you cast with them is automatically cast at that level — no need to choose a slot level.

Mystic Arcanum. At 11th level, you gain a single 6th-level slot (1/long rest). At 13th, 15th, and 17th levels you gain 7th, 8th, and 9th level slots (also 1/long rest). These are separate from your Pact Magic slots.

Multiclassing. Pact Magic slots are tracked separately. Pact Magic slot level is determined by Warlock level alone. For regular spell slots from other classes, use the multiclass spellcasting table (PHB p.165) — exclude your Warlock levels.

Eldritch Invocations. Many invocations enhance Pact Magic — additional spells known (at-will or once/rest), or modifications to eldritch blast.

▸ How Full Casting works (PHB Ch.10)

Full progression. Your spell slots advance every level — you gain access to higher-level spell slots as you level up, reaching up to 9th-level spells at level 17.

Cantrip scaling. Cantrip damage scales with character level, not class level: 5th (2x), 11th (3x), 17th (4x). PHB p.201

Spell slots recharge on a long rest. You can convert between slots using class features like Sorcery Points or Arcane Recovery.

Higher-level slots. Casting a spell with a higher-level slot can increase its damage, targets, or duration — check the spell's "At Higher Levels" section.

{% endif %} {% if caster_type == "half" %}
▸ How Half Casting works (PHB p.164)

Half progression. Your spell slots advance at half the rate of a full caster — you get 1st-level slots at level 2, 2nd-level at level 5, and max out at 5th-level slots at level 17.

Paladins & Rangers are prepared casters — you choose which spells to prepare each day from the full class list. Your preparation cap = level + spellcasting modifier. PHB p.84

Artificers are also prepared casters, but they use Intelligence and have their own spell list. TCoE

Multiclassing. Half caster levels count as half toward the multiclass spellcaster table (rounded down). Paladin 4 = 2 caster levels. PHB p.164

▸ How Multiclass Casting works (PHB p.164-165)

Combined table. Your total spell slots are determined by adding up your caster levels from each class:

  • Full caster (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard): 1 level per class level
  • Half caster (Paladin, Ranger, Artificer): ½ level per class level (rounded down)
  • Pact Magic (Warlock): not included — tracked separately

Spells known/prepared are per-class: you learn and prepare spells for each class independently using that class's rules.

Spellcasting ability is per-class: Wizard uses Intelligence, Cleric uses Wisdom. Your spell save DC and attack bonus are calculated separately for each class's spells.

Pact Magic from Warlock levels is not affected by the multiclass table. You track Pact Magic slots separately from your regular spell slots.

▸ Spellcasting Rules (PHB Ch.10)

Casting Time. Spells require 1 action, 1 bonus action, 1 reaction, or longer. Bonus Action Rule: you can only cast one non-cantrip spell per turn. If you cast any spell as a bonus action (even a cantrip), the only other spell you can cast that turn is a cantrip with a casting time of 1 action. PHB p.202

Range. Touch, Self, or distance in feet. PHB p.202-203

Components. Verbal (V), Somatic (S — free hand), Material (M). A spellcasting focus replaces non-costly, non-consumed materials. PHB p.203

Duration. Instantaneous, timed, or Concentration (up to duration). One concentration spell at a time. Damage forces CON save (DC 10 or half damage, whichever higher). Lost if incapacitated/killed. PHB p.203-204

Targets & Areas of Effect. Cone, Cube, Cylinder, Line, Sphere. Effect spreads in straight lines; blocked by total cover. PHB p.204-205

Saving Throws. Spell Save DC = 8 + proficiency + spellcasting mod. PHB p.205

Attack Rolls. Spell Attack Modifier = proficiency + spellcasting mod. PHB p.205

Combining Effects. Same spell cast multiple times don't combine; most potent/recent applies. Different spells coexist. PHB p.205

Higher Levels. Upcasting uses that slot's effective level. Cantrips scale with character level, not slots. PHB p.201

Rituals. Bard, Cleric, Druid, Wizard can cast ritual-tagged spells without a slot — adds 10 minutes. PHB p.201-202
