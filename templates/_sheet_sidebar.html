Each class grants proficiency in two saves. The DC is set by the effect (e.g. a spell's DC = 8 + caster's proficiency + ability mod). Usually a success means no harm or reduced harm.

Damage Resistance — halves damage of that type (PHB p.197). If you have resistance to a type, you take half damage from that source (rounded down). Multiple resistances don't stack — you still take half damage.

Damage Immunity — negates all damage of that type entirely. No damage from that source.

Damage Vulnerability — doubles damage of that type. Very rare, usually from curses or special monster traits (PHB p.197).

Condition Immunity — immune to that condition's effects. Magic can't put an elf to sleep, poison can't paralyze a creature immune to poisoned (PHB p.290-292).

Damage types: acid, bludgeoning, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, piercing, poison, psychic, radiant, slashing, thunder.
