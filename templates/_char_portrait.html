{# Shared character portrait tile. Portraits live in `characters.portrait_url` as multi-MB base64 data URLs, so every render site goes through the cacheable image route (see routes/characters/sheet.py::character_portrait_image) and asks for a thumbnail sized for the slot. Characters without a portrait get a dashed tile with their initial so rows keep their alignment. Pass `has_portrait` (a boolean — prefer a SQL `portrait_url IS NOT NULL AND portrait_url != ''` expression so the base64 blob never enters the template).
