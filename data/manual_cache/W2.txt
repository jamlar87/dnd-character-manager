MAGICAL WINES
OF THE RIVER COURT
Kept in cool, dark underground chambers
below the Neurabon river, the wine reserved
for magical enchantment is aged at least
twenty years before being removed. The
brackish cellars, filled with racks of dark
wine, are kept clean by a variety of snail
loyal to King Ulorian.

Wondrous item (wine), common
The white glass of this bottle of sweet, dark
red wine is made from the sand found along
the banks of the Neurabon river. It contains
enough for six servings and bears the sigil of
Ulorian the First, King of the River Court.
