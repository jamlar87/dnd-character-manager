                                                Dues for the Dead
                   For years, the Most Solemn Order of the Silent Shroud has tended the dead at Valinghen graveyard, providing them a
                   peaceful eternal rest. Now, that rest has been disturbed by a necromancer seeking out a key to re-activate the Pool of
                   Radiance. An adventure for 1st-4th level characters.
